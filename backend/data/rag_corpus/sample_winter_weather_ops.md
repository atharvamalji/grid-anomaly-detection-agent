## Extreme Cold Weather Events

Extended periods of extreme cold increase electric demand for heating while
simultaneously risking generator and fuel-supply disruptions (frozen
equipment, gas curtailments). Historical winter storms have shown that
demand can exceed forecast by a wide margin during multi-day cold snaps,
producing sustained hourly demand well above the same-hour-prior-week
baseline.

## Operator Response Actions

During cold weather events, operators typically: issue conservative
operations advisories to generation owners ahead of the event, increase
reserve requirements, coordinate with neighboring balancing authorities on
emergency energy transfers, and closely monitor generator availability for
weather-related outages.

## Post-Event Review

After a significant weather event, operators conduct a review comparing
actual demand and generation performance against forecasts and reserve
margins, to inform future extreme-weather preparedness.
